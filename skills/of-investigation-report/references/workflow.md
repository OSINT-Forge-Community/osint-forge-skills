# Write an investigation report notes

The generator renders recorded claims, inventory and limits. It does not discover factual conclusions from unexamined pieces. Analysts check semantic support before recording them.

Literal observations, supported inferences and unconfirmed candidates have different states. Retain inconvenient contradictory pieces and explain why a conclusion survives or must weaken.

Qualitative confidence needs rationale. Do not claim calibrated probability, expert certification, judicial admissibility or absolute authenticity. Integrity checks are distinct from these conclusions.

Reports can contain personal data. Markdown or hashes do not make them safe to share. Benchmark results name actual client/model, fixture and skill versions; unavailable trials remain not_run.
