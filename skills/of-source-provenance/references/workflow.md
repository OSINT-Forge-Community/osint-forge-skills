# Check source provenance notes

Assess capture fidelity, direct access to the event and independence separately. Do not collapse these into a percentage.

A press release repeated by five publications is one origin for its assertion. A publication can add an independently observed detail: assess the specific claim rather than the whole article.

Exact byte matches prove a captured-file duplicate, not common ownership or publication order. Footer years do not date an article. Search snippets and generated summaries are leads, not verification of the page.

The helper merges byte duplicates and supplied origin labels transitively. Independence between resulting groups remains unknown and requires analyst evidence.
