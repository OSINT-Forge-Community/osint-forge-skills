# Audit competing hypotheses notes

For each meaningful candidate record support, contradictions, assumptions, predicted observations and a failure condition. A syntactically valid evidence citation does not establish semantic support.

Multiple models using the same inputs or initial narrative are not independent witnesses. Try removing shared-origin reports and uncertain assumptions to expose fragile conclusions.

Negative evidence needs a coverage argument. Missing logs can reflect retention, filtering, clock skew or an incomplete export. Later disclosure can revise an interpretation without proving the earlier analyst could know it.

The helper checks missing references and simple state contradictions. It does not prove entailment or calibrate probabilities. Use qualitative confidence with a concrete rationale.

## Preserve dependency and knowledge context

Record `--depends-on` only for conclusions actually used as premises. After a
correction, inspect `review-status` and re-evaluate affected descendants using
new claims rather than rewriting old ones. Record when material was available;
a later disclosure cannot justify an earlier decision retroactively. For each
candidate, record which observation would distinguish it from its strongest
plausible competitor. Compatibility alone does not add affirmative support.

## Structured alternatives and tests

Use schema 4 hypothesis records for candidate statements, predictions and
explicit alternatives, then test records for action, expected observation,
state and outcome. Follow the [validated record recipes](recipes.md#hypotheses-and-tests).
A completed test requires its actual observation and E references. A blocked
test is a gap. Preserve inconclusive results without converting them into
support or contradiction. Revise or retire records by appending a complete
superseding record with a rationale. These states are not calibrated confidence.
